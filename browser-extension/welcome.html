Connect the app

  1. Install or open Download Craft (download it here).
  2. In the app open Settings → Browser Integration and press Install next to this browser.
  3. Restart the browser, then press Check again above.

Until then the extension still works on its own: pick Download in browser on any video it finds.

How it works

  1. Downloads you start in the browser go to the app (paused in the browser first, so nothing is lost if the app says no).
  2. Hold Alt while clicking a link to keep that one download in the browser (changeable in the app's Capture settings).
  3. Videos: a small Download this video bar appears on players; the toolbar button lists every stream with its quality and size.
  4. Right-click a page → Download all links with Download Craft to pick files from it.
